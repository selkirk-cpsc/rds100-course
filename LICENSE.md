## Changes made to the UBC material

The UBC DSCI 100 material was adapted for an asynchronous online section delivered
through Moodle and JupyterHub. Lectures were split and extended into 24 notebooks,
enriched with new figures, executed code and fragments, and cut into short video
segments. UBC course administration was removed and replaced with Selkirk policy.
Tutorials and assignments were revised and re-keyed, database and live-web sections
were replaced with saved snapshots, and the assessment scheme was rewritten.
