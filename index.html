RDS 100: Introduction to Data Science

Schedule · Data sets · Textbook · JupyterHub (link coming soon) · Moodle · Licence

RDS 100 is taught online. The lesson videos, check questions, grades and exact due dates are on Moodle. The tutorials and assignments are notebooks on JupyterHub (link coming soon). This site has the slides, the data sets used in the lessons, and a link to the free online textbook, Data Science: A First Introduction with Python.

Tutorials and assignments. Most weeks have two notebooks on JupyterHub.

  • The tutorial is a code-along: you work through it while the tutorial video works through it on screen. It is graded for completion, not correctness.
  • The assignment is done on your own, on a data set you have not seen. It is the week's real mark, graded on correctness.

Do the tutorial first: it is the warm-up for the assignment. Both are due Sunday at 23:59 at the end of the following week. Week 12 is the exception: its notebooks are due at the end of week 12, because Midterm 2 comes next.

Notebooks are numbered by week, so there is no 06 or 13 (review and midterm weeks), and week 5 (Git) has a tutorial but no assignment.

Slides appear here as each week opens. Slides is the slide show (use the arrow keys); Page is the same deck as one long page, easier to search and print.
